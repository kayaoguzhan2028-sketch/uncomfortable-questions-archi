# Whose Stadium? The World Cup, Architecture, Power, Football | Uncomfortable Answers in Architecture #1

We are starting a new video podcast series on YouTube — to make academic work in
architecture and neighbouring disciplines visible in public life, to bring it
into current political argument, and to talk about space.

Right after the World Cup, we are taking up the space of football: the stadium.
Is a stadium a site of rent and control, or a common space where people who do
not know one another gather around a shared feeling?

In the first episode of Uncomfortable Answers in Architecture, we talk with Ali
Onur Demirçalı about stadiums through architecture, the city, power, labour and
belonging.
